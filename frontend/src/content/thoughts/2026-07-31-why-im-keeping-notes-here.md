I figure out something interesting most weeks — at work, in a class, or messing with a side project at 1am — and then I lose it. It goes in my notes app, the notes app becomes a graveyard, and six months later I re-learn the same thing from scratch.

So this is the fix. When something is worth thinking through properly, I write it here.

## What goes here

Whatever I'm actually thinking about, roughly:

- engineering things I had to work out the hard way, written down before I forget the details
- what I'm learning at Klaviyo and at Northeastern, minus anything I shouldn't share
- side projects, including the ones that didn't work
- occasional opinions I'm willing to defend

No schedule. I'd rather post four things a year I still stand behind than something every week.

## How it works

Each entry is a markdown file in this site's repo. I write, commit, and it shows up here — no CMS, no dashboard, nothing to log into. That's deliberate: if publishing takes more than a couple of minutes I know I'll stop doing it.

If you want to argue with something I wrote, my email is at the bottom of the home page.
